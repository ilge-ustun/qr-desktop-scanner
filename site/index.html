How it works

  1. 1Press ⇧⌘2Or click the QR in the menu bar. Change the shortcut by clicking it in the app.
  2. 2Drag a boxThe Mac's own screenshot crosshair. Around a code on any screen, in any app. Esc cancels.
  3. 3Read, then decideSee the link, text or Wi-Fi network. Copy it, open it, or join the network. Nothing happens until you click.

Checks the link before you do

A QR code is a link from a stranger. QR Scanner shows where it really goes and marks what looks wrong:

  • Fake destinations: paypal.com@evil.example really goes to evil.example.
  • Look-alike letters in the address, like a Cyrillic а in apple.
  • Unencrypted links, bare IP addresses and link shorteners.
  • Script and file links that can't be opened at all.

Wi-Fi codes show the network and keep the password hidden until you ask. Join with one click.

▲ About the first launch

macOS will warn you once

QR Scanner isn't signed with an Apple Developer ID yet, as this is a free side project. So the first time you open it, macOS says it "could not verify" the app. To open it anyway:

  1. Drag QR Scanner from the DMG to Applications, and open it. Click Done on the warning.
  2. Open System Settings → Privacy & Security, scroll down and click Open Anyway.
  3. Enter your Mac password, then click Open. It opens normally from then on.

On the first scan it asks for Screen Recording permission. It needs it to read the box you drag, and it never records anything else.
